The challenge

Why AI programmes stall

Most public sector AI deployments fail not because the tools are weak, but because the foundations underneath them were never checked. Governance without an owner. Data nobody has profiled. A workforce that hears about it late. This assessment surfaces those gaps before they cost you a programme.

Scope and limitations

This is a self-assessment. It is a fast, structured way to surface where your foundations are thin and to give a board a shared vocabulary. It is not a formal assurance product, does not replace a DPIA, DCB0160 clinical safety case, DTAC submission or equality impact assessment, and does not constitute legal or regulatory advice.
