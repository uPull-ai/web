# Security policy

uPull.ai takes the security of its website and services seriously. If you think you have found a vulnerability, please tell us privately so we can fix it before it is made public.

## How to report

Please do not open a public issue or post details publicly until we have fixed the problem.

Use either route:

- **GitHub private reporting.** Open the Security tab of this repository and choose "Report a vulnerability".
- **Email.** Write to security@upull.ai.

Please include:

- what you found and where;
- the steps to reproduce it;
- what you think the impact is;
- how to contact you, if you would like a reply.

You do not need to send exploit code. Please do not include personal data. If you come across personal data, stop and tell us.

## What to expect

- We will acknowledge your report within 3 working days. We work Monday to Thursday.
- We will give you a first assessment within 10 working days.
- We will keep you updated, and tell you when the problem is fixed.
- We will credit you if you would like us to.

## Scope

In scope: the public uPull.ai website (https://upull.ai) and the code in this repository.

The uPull.ai Academy and the client portal are going through independent testing. If you find a problem in either, please report it in the same way. We will treat it seriously, but we cannot yet confirm that they are open to research.

## Please do not

- access, change or delete data that is not yours, including any learner or client personal data;
- run denial of service tests, send spam, or target our staff or users with social engineering;
- test further than you need to show that the problem exists.

## What we do not offer

We do not run a bug bounty and cannot pay rewards. We do not intend to take action against people who act in good faith and follow this policy. This policy is not a legal agreement and does not give permission to break the law.
