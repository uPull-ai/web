About the Hayes Village Residents Association

The Hayes Village Residents Association (HVRA) is the independent, resident-led voice for everyone living in Hayes Village. We exist to make daily life easier, fairer, and more connected by giving residents a clear channel to raise issues, share information, and work together on practical improvements. We hold regular meetings, publish updates on local issues, and act as a constructive link between residents and the organisations that shape our neighbourhood, including developers, managing agents, the council, and emergency services. Whether helping resolve building or estate problems, pushing for better services and safety, or creating opportunities to meet neighbours and build community, HVRA supports residents, amplifies local concerns, and helps turn Hayes Village into a place where people feel informed, listened to, and proud to live.

Bike storage

Bike storage in Hayes Village is provided in a dedicated communal area within the car park. The bike store is a secure, fob-access space designed for residents to keep their bicycles off balconies, corridors, and communal walkways.

  • Location — The bike storage area is located on the ground level in the car park, clearly signed and accessible from the main vehicle entrance.
  • Access — Entry to the bike store is via your resident access fob. Only residents with a valid fob can enter the storage area.
  • Use — The space is intended for bicycle storage only. Please do not store e-scooters, motorbikes, boxes, or other items unless explicitly permitted by the managing agent.
  • Security — While the store is fob-access controlled, residents are encouraged to use their own bike lock as an extra precaution.
  • Issues and maintenance — Report any problems with the bike store (lighting, access, damage, or misuse) to FirstPort customer services or via the estate management contact details.

Bins & recycling

Bins and recycling in Hayes Village (UB3 4) are managed by Hillingdon Council. The communal bin store for each building is located on the ground floor at the bottom of the block, and residents need their access fob to enter the store and dispose of rubbish and recycling. Collection days vary by exact address, so residents should use the council's "Look up my collection day" tool with their postcode to confirm their specific schedule.

  • Bin collection (UB3 4) — General waste, recycling, and garden waste are collected Monday to Friday from early morning. Use the council's online tool to check your exact collection day and any bank holiday changes. Hillingdon — Look up my collection day
  • Bulky waste collection — For large items such as sofas, mattresses, or fridges, residents can book a paid bulky waste collection with Hillingdon Council. Current fees are around £53 for residents, £39 for over-65s, and £94 for landlords, with collections Monday to Friday. Hillingdon — Bulky waste collection
  • Fly-tipping reporting — To report dumped rubbish or illegal waste in or around Hayes Village, use the council's online fly-tipping report form, providing the location and details so the council can investigate and arrange removal. Hillingdon — Report fly-tipping

Residents should place their household bins in the communal bin store and ensure lids are closed. For large items that do not fit, the council's bulky waste service or approved private removers should be used instead of leaving items in or near the bin store.

Council tax

Council tax in Hayes Village is charged by Hillingdon Council and funds local services such as waste collection, libraries, parks, and adult social care. New residents should register for council tax as soon as they move in, either by setting up an online council tax account or by contacting the council with their move-in date and property details. Bills are based on the property's valuation band (A to H), with Band D in Hillingdon around £1,952 per year for 2026/27 before any discounts or reductions.

  • Applying and managing council tax — Create an online account to view bills, set up direct debit, update contact details, and apply for discounts. Hillingdon – Your online council tax account
  • Council tax bands and rates — Charges depend on the property's 1991 valuation band, with Hillingdon and Greater London Authority portions shown on each bill. Hillingdon – How much is my council tax?
  • Single person discount — If only one adult lives in the home as their main residence, they may be eligible for a 25% single person discount, applied for through the online account.
  • Council Tax Reduction (support) — Households on low income or certain benefits, such as Universal Credit or Pension Credit, may qualify. Working-age applicants generally need savings below £6,000, and pension-age applicants below £16,000 (unless on Pension Credit). Hillingdon – Council Tax Reduction Scheme
  • Payments and help — Pay by direct debit, online, or via the 24-hour payment line on 01895 556699. Residents struggling to pay should email counciltax@hillingdon.gov.uk to discuss options.

New occupants should notify Hillingdon Council when they move in or out, and check their bill for the correct band and any applicable discounts or reductions.

Electoral registration & voting

Electoral registration ensures you are on the electoral roll and able to vote in UK elections and referendums. You must register at each address where you live, so when you move into Hayes Village you need to either register for the first time or update your existing registration with your new address.

  • Register to vote — Add your name to the electoral register online. You will need your name, date of birth, address, and National Insurance number; it takes about five minutes. GOV.UK – Register to vote
  • Moving address — If you are already registered elsewhere, you must re-register at your new Hayes Village address using the same online form with your new details and previous address. Hillingdon – Electoral services query
  • Deadlines — Your name must be on the register at least 12 working days before an election or referendum for you to vote in that poll, so register as soon as you move in. Hillingdon – Your guide to voting
  • Open register option — You can choose whether to be on the "open register" (a version sold to companies); this is optional and does not affect your right to vote. Electoral Commission – Register to vote
  • Hillingdon Council support — For questions about registration status, postal or proxy voting, contact Hillingdon's Electoral Services via their online query form. Hillingdon – Electoral services query

Residents should re-register every time they move, including when moving within Hayes Village or between blocks, to keep their details up to date.

Parking

Parking in Hayes Village is controlled and permitted only for residents and registered visitors. Residents must hold a valid resident parking permit for their allocated spaces, while all visitor parking is managed digitally through the Virtual Visitor Parking (VVP) app operated by PCM UK (Parking Control Management). Visitors cannot park without an active virtual permit, and unauthorised vehicles are subject to enforcement and penalty charges.

Visitor parking via the Virtual Visitor app

  • How it works — Residents download the VirtualVisitorPCM app (iOS or Android), register their tenancy details, and enter their visitor's vehicle registration to start a paid parking session. There are no paper permits or scratch cards. Virtual Visitor Parking
  • Costs — Typical visitor sessions are priced around £6.99 for 30 hours and £7.99 for 48 hours, with rates shown in the app at time of booking.
  • Where to get the app — Search "VirtualVisitorPCM" in the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. VirtualVisitorPCM — App Store

Resident parking permits

  • Resident-only spaces — On-site parking is restricted to residents with valid permits and their registered visitors.
  • Applying for permits — Apply through the PCM permit portal (PermitPCM), providing proof of residency and vehicle details — you may need to upload a tenancy agreement or completion statement. PermitPCM — Parking Permits

Fines and Penalty Charge Notices (PCNs)

  • Enforcement — Vehicles parked without a valid resident permit or active visitor session may receive a Penalty Charge Notice (PCN).
  • Typical charges — Exact amounts for Hayes Village are set by the site's operator; private PCNs in similar schemes often start around £60–£70, reduced by 50% if paid within 14 days. Always check the amount on the notice you receive.
  • Payment — PCNs must be paid by the deadline stated on the notice, usually within 28 days of issue. Late payment can lead to increased charges and further enforcement.

Appeals process

  • Informal appeal — If you believe a PCN was issued incorrectly, you can make an informal challenge, usually within 14 days of the notice (or 21 days if posted). Do not pay the PCN if you intend to appeal, as payment is treated as acceptance of liability. Citizens Advice — Appealing a parking ticket
  • Formal representation — If your informal appeal is rejected, you will receive a Notice to Owner and have 28 days to make a formal representation with evidence (photos, permits, booking confirmations).
  • Independent tribunal — If the formal appeal is rejected, you can escalate to an independent adjudicator such as the Traffic Penalty Tribunal or the relevant private parking appeals service, depending on the operator's accreditation. GOV.UK — Challenge a parking fine

Residents should ensure all visitors are booked via the Virtual Visitor app before they arrive, and keep booking confirmations until the visit is complete, as these are key evidence if a PCN is disputed.

Security & CCTV

Hayes Village has on-site security measures and CCTV coverage in communal areas to help deter crime and support investigations. The system is managed by the estate's managing agent, and footage is not accessible to residents. In the event of a crime or incident, the Metropolitan Police can request access to relevant CCTV as part of their investigation.

How to report an incident

  • Emergency — If there is immediate danger to life, a crime in progress, or a serious incident, call 999 straight away.
  • Non-emergency — For crimes that are not in progress, suspicious activity, or general enquiries, call 101 or report online. Met Police — Contact us
  • Hayes Town Safer Neighbourhoods Team — For local policing issues and community concerns, email HayesTown@contact.metengage.co.uk (do not use this address to report crimes).
  • Textphone / specialist services — For hearing or speech impairments, use 18001 101 (non-emergency) or pre-register for emergency SMS/text services; BSL users can access the 999 BSL service. GOV.UK — Contact the police

Contacting FirstPort (Hayes Village management)

  • Customer Services — Call 0333 321 4080 (Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm) or use the online contact form. FirstPort — Contact us
  • Out-of-hours emergency support — FirstPort provides 24/7 support for urgent estate issues; the out-of-hours number is listed on your welcome pack and estate notices.
  • Email — For non-urgent queries, email help@firstport.co.uk.

Do not attempt to tackle crime yourself. If you see suspicious activity, a break-in, or anything that feels unsafe, keep a safe distance, do not confront anyone, and report it immediately to the police on 999 (emergency) or 101 (non-emergency). CCTV footage is retained for a limited period and can only be accessed by the police and authorised parties for investigations, so report incidents promptly.

Water

Water services in Hayes Village are split between two suppliers: Independent Water Networks (IWNL) for clean drinking water and Thames Water for wastewater and sewerage. IWNL owns and operates the local water network that supplies homes with clean water, handling metering, supply queries, and clean water issues. Thames Water is responsible for removing and treating wastewater, dealing with drains, sewers, and any waste water problems.

  • Clean water (IWNL) — Supplies drinking water and manages meters and clean water queries. Phone: 02920 028711. IWNL contact
  • Wastewater (Thames Water) — Manages drains, sewers, and wastewater services. Phone: 0800 316 9800 (24/7 for water and waste issues). Thames Water contact
