Why AI programmes stall

Most public-sector AI programmes do not fail because the tools are weak. They fail because the foundations underneath them were never checked.

Governance without an owner

Policies exist on paper. No one is accountable for them in practice.

Data nobody has profiled

Everyone assumes the data is ready. It rarely is.

Workforce that hears about it late

Staff discover the change when it is already decided. Resistance is rational.

Assurance that arrives after the pilot

Safety and clinical risk are treated as afterthoughts instead of design inputs.

This assessment surfaces those gaps early — while you can still do something about them. It also shows where your organisation is already strong, so you can build on it.

How it works
