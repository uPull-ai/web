Why this community exists

Most AI programmes fail because the people who will actually use the tools are only brought in at the end. We reverse that.

Share what worked (and what didn’t)

Talk honestly without the usual filters. Real stories from people doing the same job.

Ask practical questions

Get answers from peers who have already tried ambient documentation, referral automation or triage redesign.

Find your people

Connect with others who are redesigning workflows rather than just “adopting technology”.

Turn experiments into learning

Isolated pilots become shared knowledge. What works in one trust can travel.

How it works

Simple structure, high signal.

  • Open forum with clear channels (documentation, referrals, triage, ambient, robotics, governance)
  • Monthly live sessions led by frontline staff who have already run a pull
  • Peer support for people preparing a 6-week proof-of-value
  • Direct line into the uGrowX learning pathways
For leaders
Use the community to grow a cohort of confident intrapreneurs inside your organisation instead of relying on external consultants for every step.
