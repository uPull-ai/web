## Full description

> uPull.ai helps NHS trusts, Integrated Care Boards, and wider UK health-and-care
> organisations adopt AI without the common failure mode of buying technology before
> understanding the workflow it's meant to fix.
>
> The company's methodology — "the Pull principle" — inverts the usual approach:
> workflow first, AI second. Rather than pushing a generic AI tool into an
> organisation, uPull.ai starts by identifying one real, measurable operational or
> clinical bottleneck alongside the frontline team, redesigns that workflow with
> staff who do the job every day, defines a safety envelope for governance and
> oversight, and only then introduces the AI capability suited to that redesigned
> process. The goal of each engagement is a credible, narrow proof-of-value pilot —
> typically run over four to six weeks — that produces measured evidence (staff
> time, cost avoidance, capacity released) rather than an untested, organisation-wide
> rollout.
>
> uPull.ai also runs uGrowX, a free CPD-recognised learning programme for NHS and
> public sector staff covering agentic, clinical, robotic and ambient AI, and
> uCollabX, a compliance-checked registry connecting NHS staff with AI vendors whose
> solutions have been verified against safety, data protection, interoperability,
> usability and evidence standards.
>
> uPull.ai is based in London, UK.
