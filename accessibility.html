Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Our commitment

uPull.ai wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including people with visual, hearing, motor, or cognitive impairments. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA — the standard NHS and public sector partners expect and that UK public sector bodies are required to meet under the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018.

uPull.ai Ltd is a private company and these regulations don't apply to us directly — but because our clients and audience are NHS and public sector organisations that must meet them, we hold ourselves to the same bar.

Current status

This statement is a good-faith self-assessment, not the result of a full independent audit. We believe the site is partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 AA. Known areas we're still working through:

  • Some decorative images rely on alt="" and graceful degradation rather than a full audit of every image's accessible name
  • The AI Readiness Assessment's radar chart is rendered as SVG with a text-equivalent bar chart alongside it, but hasn't been tested with a screen reader end-to-end
  • Colour contrast has been designed to AA levels throughout, but hasn't been verified with automated tooling against every component

We're addressing these as we go and will update this statement as issues are fixed or found.

What we've done

  • Built the site with semantic HTML and visible focus states on interactive elements
  • Designed the colour palette to meet WCAG AA contrast ratios on both light and dark backgrounds
  • Made all pages responsive and usable at any zoom level or viewport size
  • Ensured the site works with keyboard navigation alone (no mouse-only interactions)

Feedback and contact

If you find any part of this site you can't access, or something that should work better with assistive technology, please tell us: support@upull.ai. We aim to acknowledge accessibility feedback within 5 working days and will let you know how we plan to address it.

Technical information

This site is built as static HTML/CSS/JavaScript, without a client-side framework, and hosted on GitHub Pages. This statement was last reviewed on the date shown at the top of this page and will be reviewed again at least annually or whenever the site changes materially.
