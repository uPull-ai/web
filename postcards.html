Why a postcard

Short, specific, and easy to say yes to

Most "future of the NHS" content is vague because the question is vague. A postcard from 2030 forces something concrete: one decision, one relic, one piece of advice — in under a minute.

Every postcard is read by the uPull.ai team before it goes on the public wall, and only the person who wrote it — or the uPull.ai admin team — can ever take it down. Everyone else can read, nobody else can edit or remove.

Before you write

Four prompts to get you there

You don't have to answer all four in your postcard — they're there to help you find the specific thing worth saying.

01

The Frame

It's 2030. What single decision made around 2026 transformed your service?

02

The Relic

What 2026 practice or workflow are teams shocked we used to accept?

03

The Catalyst

What was the biggest barrier your team had to overcome to make that shift?

04

The Advice

What should NHS leaders in 2026 start doing tomorrow?
