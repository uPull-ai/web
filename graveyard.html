Instructions

How this works

It takes about three minutes, and you never have to say who you are.

  1. Tell us what it was meant to doOne sentence. That sentence becomes the inscription on the headstone.
  2. Give us the shape, not the identityOrganisation type, specialty, a cost band, roughly how long it ran. Never the name of a trust, a supplier or a person.
  3. Say what you think killed itPick from the list, or write your own. You can pick more than one.
  4. Answer the two questions everyone skipsWho actually ran it day to day, and could your own team have kept it going without the supplier.
  5. A human reads it before anyone else doesWe check every entry by hand, twice a day. Anything identifying is removed, or the entry is rejected.
  6. Your headstone goes upUsually within a day. Then it becomes part of the pattern.

What we do with it

At the end of the campaign we publish The Graveyard Report: every cause of death, with counts rather than percentages, and the full method and limitations written out.

It goes out free, to everyone, whether you have ever spoken to us or not. It is not gated behind a form and there is no call to book to read it.

If we ever quote a rate from this data, we publish the denominator next to it. "48% engagement" on 64 impressions is 31 actions, and we would rather say both.

What we will not do with it

We will not sell it, licence it, or use it to build a target list. Submitting a headstone does not put you on a mailing list and does not mean anyone will contact you.

The only exception is the optional box at the very end, where you can ask us to come back to you. That one is opt-in, and it is genuinely optional.

The rules

Three absolutes

These are not guidelines. If we cannot hold them, we take the wall down.

  • No organisation is ever named.Not the trust, not the ICB, not the region if the region would make it obvious.
  • No supplier or product is ever named.This wall is about a pattern, not about blame, and naming vendors would turn it into something else entirely.
  • No individual is ever named.Not the executive who cancelled it, not the person who left.

How moderation actually works

Nothing publishes automatically. Every submission sits in a queue until a named person at uPull.ai has read it. We do two passes a day, at 08:00 and 16:00.

If an entry names an organisation, a supplier or a person, we remove that detail. If the combination of specialty, cost band and timeframe would make the organisation obvious to someone in the sector, we either generalise it or reject the entry. When we are unsure, we reject.

If you think an entry identifies you

Email info@upull.ai with the words "takedown" in the subject line. We will remove it within four hours, and we will not ask you to justify the request.

What not to put in the box

Please do not enter anything patient-identifiable, commercially sensitive, or covered by a confidentiality clause. The free-text fields are read by a human, but they are not a safe place for information you are not free to share.
