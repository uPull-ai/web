Pricing

uPull.ai is priced as an enterprise transformation partner, not a low-cost consultancy. Our pricing reflects the senior expertise required to reduce risk, redesign workflows, and support adoption across frontline teams. A staged path from diagnosis to scale

Each stage builds on the last — start where the risk is lowest, and only invest further once the case is proven.

Stage A

Discovery Sprint

£35,000
4-day intensive · two senior operators

Covers organisational assessment, workflow discovery, AI readiness, and identification of wasted licence spend and operational friction. Designed to create a credible enterprise entry point and establish whether there is a strong case for deeper transformation work.

Best for
  • Organisations exploring AI adoption for the first time.
  • Teams that need to understand where existing digital investments are failing.
  • Leaders who want a low-risk way to identify high-value opportunities.
Stage B

Blueprint Enablement

£75,000–£100,000
Structured programme · 2–3 months

Supports solution blueprinting, workforce enablement, and operating model design. Suitable for organisations ready to move from diagnostic insight into a practical transformation plan.

Best for
  • Organisations ready to turn discovery into action.
  • Teams that need a clear roadmap for adoption and implementation.
  • Leaders seeking a vendor-neutral operating model and workforce enablement plan.
Stage C

Deployment and Scale

From £250,000
Base fee + 10–15% of realised savings

Supports long-term deployment, change management, governance, and scaling across multiple teams, sites, or business units. The fixed base fee covers core delivery, and the outcome-linked element aligns uPull.ai with measurable operational value.

Best for
  • Organisations ready for full transformation deployment.
  • Multi-team or multi-site programmes.
  • Leaders who want a long-term strategic partner focused on measurable value.
