What the Rollout Numbers Can Tell Us

Agentic, Robotics, Ambient & Clinical AI. Every week, uPull.ai tracks four categories of AI moving through the NHS: a headline, a use case, a governance note and a workflow to try. What happens when an AI agent strays outside its brief? And what can an NHS team learn from a theatre milestone, a patient’s account of an AI scribe and a national rise in scan volumes? This week’s stories put different kinds of evidence side by side, with a practical check to try in each workflow.

1 Agentic AI

Headline AI development

A government factsheet updated 3 November says the government intends to introduce four measures on agentic loss of control, following consultation: security and resilience requirements, affected party notification, evidence preservation requirements for regulated entities that report an incident, and post-incident review powers for Ministers to task technical experts. The measures are proposals, to be brought into force through secondary legislation following Royal Assent. Primary source quoted

Source: Department for Science, Innovation and Technology factsheet
Healthcare or NHS use case

In May, Sandwell and West Birmingham NHS Trust announced a radiology referral vetting pilot for selected ultrasound and MRI referrals, with radiologist oversight. Its announcement gave 16 October 2026 as the planned end date. It illustrates where an agent can sit in a real decision chain. The launch notice does not establish the pilot’s results or whether it continued. Primary source quoted

Source: Sandwell and West Birmingham NHS Trust pilot announcement
Governance or ethics note

The factsheet’s phrase “evidence preservation requirements” describes a proposal. The practical question for an NHS team today is whether it can reconstruct what an agent accessed or changed, when a human intervened and what happened after the intervention. Decide what operational evidence is needed and who can access it. Primary source quoted

Source: Government factsheet, proposed measures
Try this in a workflow

Give a test agent one fictional referral outside its permitted scope. Check whether it stops, routes the case to the named reviewer and leaves a record of the attempted action. Agree who can pause the workflow if it fails the test.

2 Robotics AI

Headline AI development

On 6 November, London North West University Healthcare NHS Trust reported more than 350 robotic-assisted procedures across Ealing and Northwick Park since adding two robots in April. The trust describes a programme spanning five surgical specialties across the two sites. Primary source quoted

Source: LNWH six-month programme update
Healthcare or NHS use case

Ealing carried out more than 250 of the reported procedures and Northwick Park more than 100. The trust describes Ealing as the high-volume hub and Northwick Park as the centre for more complex robotic procedures. Those are two different operating models within one programme. Primary source quoted

Source: LNWH site breakdown
Governance or ethics note

The trust’s article says Ealing’s start “is believed to represent the strongest start to a new robotic surgery programme across the whole of Europe.” Its article provides local activity counts but does not give a European comparator dataset. Use the counts as evidence of throughput. Ask separately for procedure mix, complications, conversion to open surgery and patient outcomes before judging comparative benefit. Primary source quoted

Source: LNWH programme update
Try this in a workflow

Review one robotic theatre list with the theatre lead. Record set-up and turnover times alongside case mix and cancellations, then compare with a comparable list. Ask where the programme needs more training or theatre capacity before adding another specialty.

3 Ambient AI

Headline AI development

On 5 November, The Christie published a new patient account from its ambient scribing rollout, which also featured in our 27 October edition. The new detail is how the encounter felt to a patient in supportive oncology. The trust says rollout across outpatient services is under way. Primary source quoted

Source: The Christie patient account
Healthcare or NHS use case

Karen Booth describes her consultant being able to focus on her without having to look at a keyboard. She recalls, “I remember my consultant asking my permission, and then I forgot it was there.” That is a patient’s account of one consultation. The trust says early results show the technology can help clinical correspondence reach patients and GPs more quickly, and gives no measured effect in this release. Primary source quoted

Source: The Christie patient account
Governance or ethics note

The Christie says patients are asked before the tool is used and clinicians check and approve its output. NHS England says the registry supports local procurement and assurance, and that assurance and decision-making sit with the local NHS organisation. Test the patient explanation and the manual path when someone declines, alongside the checking step for the draft note. Primary source quoted

Sources: The Christie rollout information · NHS England supplier registry
Try this in a workflow

Run a fictional clinic appointment with two branches, one where the patient accepts the scribe and one where they decline. Have the team demonstrate both paths and identify who checks and signs the clinic letter in each.

4 Clinical AI

Headline AI development

On 3 November, DHSC and NHS England reported that the NHS had added 125 CT and MRI scanners between March 2024 and March 2026. Community diagnostic centres carried out nearly 2.2 million CT and MRI scans in the year to March 2026, about a million more than in the year to March 2024. The announcement also reports MRI acceleration software on 54% of compatible NHS MRI scanners. The release also says every NHS trust will be given the opportunity to upgrade compatible MRI scanners by 2029. It does not say how often the installed software is used. Primary source quoted

Source: DHSC and NHS England announcement
Healthcare or NHS use case

In an earlier example, NHS Humber Health Partnership reported installing AIR Recon Deep Learning software on existing MRI machines, reducing the time taken for each scan by a third in some cases. That is a local result for some scans, separate from the national figures. Primary source quoted

Source: Hull University Teaching Hospitals, 4 July 2025
Governance or ethics note

The government release links additional equipment and AI to expanded capacity. It does not say how much of the extra million scans came from AI. It does say AI-powered MRI technology is helping services carry out on average 14% more scans, without giving the basis for that figure. Scanner throughput, completed diagnostic reports and time to treatment are different outcomes. An imaging team evaluating acceleration software should agree which it can measure, while checking image adequacy and repeat scans. Primary source quoted

Source: DHSC and NHS England announcement
Try this in a workflow

Pick one MRI protocol with the imaging lead. Compare matched sessions before and after the change. Record scanner time, usable images, repeats and whether the freed time became additional staffed slots. Check report turnaround separately.

5 Learning & Development

One standing entry this week.

Standing entry: NHS Fellowship in Clinical AI

Application reminder. The NHS Fellowship in Clinical AI’s Cohort 6 closes at 23:45 GMT on 16 November 2026. The Fellowship’s application page gives 11 August 2027 as the start date and describes two days a week for 12 months alongside work or training. Applicants should check eligibility and arrange for their approver to submit the Approval in Principle form, which is also due by 23:45 GMT on 16 November. Primary source quoted

Source: NHS Fellowship in Clinical AI application page

From uPull.ai

Before approving the next stage of a rollout, ask the team to name the decision it needs to make and the evidence that would change it. A procedure count, a patient account and an incident log each answer a different question. Put those questions into the workflow while the service can still act on the answers.
